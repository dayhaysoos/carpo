# Landing walkthrough screenshots

Captured from the real Carpo UI on 2026-09-02, on the local launch review runtime.
The source is the licensed 18-second Charge sample documented in `../demo/README.md`.
Three six-second clips were created through the upload, trim, and encoder workflow
and reached Complete before capture. No transcript was needed for these cuts.

- `workspace.webp`: desktop editor with the selected range and three completed clips.
- `workspace-mobile.webp`: the actual narrow-screen editor, showing the video and trim controls.
- `moment.webp` and `moment-mobile.webp`: close-ups of the actual precision trim controls.
- `clips.webp` and `clips-mobile.webp`: completed clips and their real preview/export/download controls.

Screenshots were cropped and encoded as WebP for delivery; interface content was
not composited or altered. Original captures are in `test-output/launch/walkthrough-*-raw.png`.
Desktop capture viewport: 1440 × 1100. Mobile capture viewport: 430 × 1000.
The browser capture's content dimensions differ slightly from the requested viewport.
